INFINITY RING — CONCEPT OVERVIEW

Your world is the interface.

Infinity Ring explores a wearable interaction combining deliberate voice activation, visual target selection and near-field illumination. The selected appearance is R3 / Iris: graphite titanium, a recessed mechanical iris, a tactile circular bezel and restrained green status arcs.

THE PROPOSED EXPERIENCE
Touch both sides to activate voice interaction. Ask to enter visual mode. Point toward an object, adjust the target boundary, capture an image and ask about the selected area. Use a deliberate stop to end the session. Illumination is a separate mode.

THREE DISTINCT LIGHT FUNCTIONS
1. A status signal on the ring.
2. A projected selection boundary on a target surface.
3. A diffuse near-field illumination area.
An ordinary status LED does not establish adjustable target projection capability.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
Ring: sensing, touch and rotary input, audio, imaging, light and feedback.
Gateway: connectivity, permissions, session state and transfer.
Cloud intelligence: speech, image understanding, context and responses.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS
This is a future product concept. Exterior images and the exploded view are concept visualizations, not photographs of validated hardware or proof of a manufacturable assembly. Final dimensions, weight, endurance, water resistance, optical safety and strong-daylight projection have not been established. Hardware candidates, custom integration and future optical research must be distinguished.

No purchasing, availability or launch-date commitment is made.
